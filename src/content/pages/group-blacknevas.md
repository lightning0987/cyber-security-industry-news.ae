## Blacknevas is a Trigona-linked double-extortion group also tracked as Trial Recovery

Blacknevas is a ransomware group first observed in November 2024 and also tracked under the alias Trial Recovery. The family is believed to derive from Trigona ransomware. Blacknevas uses double extortion, meaning it claims to steal data as well as encrypt it, and pairs AES with RSA encryption. Its published profile lists telecommunications, manufacturing, medical and legal targets across Asia-Pacific, the UK, Italy and Lithuania.

## Blacknevas has a thin, scattered UAE record outside its usual sectors

Blacknevas ranks thirteenth on this tracker with 2 claimed UAE organisations, against 10 for the leader, LockBit 3.0. The claims split evenly between [Manufacturing](/uae-ransomware-tracker/manufacturing/) and [Education](/uae-ransomware-tracker/education/), one each. Education does not appear among the sectors in the group's published profile. The first claim is dated 27 October 2025 and the second 15 October 2026, so Blacknevas shows one gap of almost a year rather than a sustained campaign. These figures come from leak-site monitoring as of the 27 November 2026 snapshot, and each entry is an attacker assertion, not a confirmed breach.

## What this means for UAE organisations

1. Treat each Blacknevas listing as an attacker claim, and ask incident response to verify any match before acting on it.
2. Add the alias Trial Recovery to watchlists and log searches, since reporting may use either name.
3. Review detection coverage for Trigona-family ransomware behaviour, because Blacknevas is believed to derive from that family.
4. Test restoration from offline backups and monitor outbound data transfers, because Blacknevas uses double extortion with AES and RSA encryption.
5. Map the personal data held in manufacturing and education systems, and rehearse the breach notification duties under the UAE PDPL, which covers any organisation processing personal data of individuals in the UAE.
