## Manufacturing ranks sixth among UAE sectors with 8 claimed organisations

Manufacturing ranks sixth of 13 UAE sectors, just below the average of 8.3 claims per active sector. Eight distinct groups appear here, one claim each. No ransomware group has claimed a second UAE manufacturing organisation in this dataset.

## Eight groups account for 8 Manufacturing claims

Manufacturing shows one claim per group across 8 distinct operations. No ransomware group has
claimed a second UAE manufacturing organisation in this dataset. Blacknevas, Cicada3301,
DragonForce and INC Ransom each appear once, as do the remaining four.

Manufacturing claims cluster late in the record. The sector saw 1 claim in 2023 and 1 in 2024,
then 4 in 2025. The partial year 2026 stands at 2. Half the sector's total falls in 2025 alone.

## What this means for UAE organisations

1. Separate plant networks from corporate networks, because manufacturing downtime converts to
   contractual penalties faster than office downtime does.
2. Inventory the equipment that cannot be patched, then compensate with segmentation rather
   than hoping for a vendor update.
3. Map your obligations under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, which reaches employee and
   supplier records as well as customer data.
4. Rehearse running production without the systems that schedule it, since the recovery
   question is operational before it is technical.
5. Confirm that remote maintenance access used by equipment vendors is time limited and
   monitored.
6. Plan against a generalist attacker rather than a sector specialist, given that 8 groups have
   appeared once each.
7. Verify that design files and process documentation are backed up apart from the systems that
   normally serve them.
8. Agree in advance who can authorise a production halt, since that decision cannot wait for an
   investigation to finish.
