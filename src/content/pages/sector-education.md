## Education ranks twelfth among UAE sectors with 3 claimed organisations

Education sits twelfth of 13 UAE sectors and carries 2.7% of all attributed claims. The count runs
far below the average of 8.6 claims per active sector. Three data points describe no pattern, and
this page exists so the sector breakdown stays complete.

Education recorded nothing at all across 2020 to 2024. On this tracker the sector is recent rather
than persistent, and its whole record fits inside twelve months.

## What this means for UAE organisations

1. Read the count as an absence of evidence rather than as evidence of safety, because 3 claims
   cannot describe a sector.
2. Note that the record doubled between 2025 and the partial year 2026, which is the only movement
   visible here.
3. Map your obligations under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, which reaches student records
   and staff records equally.
4. Treat research partnerships and shared systems as part of the estate, since an institution
   rarely controls every system its people use.
5. Compare against [Healthcare](/uae-ransomware-tracker/healthcare/), which holds 6 claims and
   shows how quickly a small sector count can move.

A sector with 3 claims from 3 groups gives nothing to model. The useful signal would be a second
claim by the same group, and this record does not contain one.
