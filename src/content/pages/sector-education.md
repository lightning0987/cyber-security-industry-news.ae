## Education ranks last among UAE sectors with 2 claimed organisations

Education ranks thirteenth of 13 UAE sectors, the lowest count on this tracker. The figure sits far below the average of 8.3 claims per active sector. Two data points describe no pattern, and this page exists so the sector breakdown stays complete.

## Both Education claims fall within four months

Leak-site monitoring recorded the first Education claim on 11 October 2025 and the second on 27
January 2026. The sector shows 1 claim in 2025 and 1 in the partial year 2026. BQTLock and
NightSpire account for one claimed organisation each.

Two data points describe no pattern. This page exists so the sector breakdown stays complete
and so the count can be tracked as it changes. A low count is not the same as low exposure.
Education bodies hold identity records for minors and for staff alike.

## What this means for UAE organisations

1. Read 2 claims as a small sample rather than as evidence that education is a low-risk sector
   in the UAE.
2. Map your obligations under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, which reaches student,
   parent and staff records equally.
3. Establish who owns identity for students, since accounts that outlive enrolment are a
   standing exposure.
4. Separate teaching networks from administrative systems, because the two have different users
   and different risk.
5. Confirm that research and examination data is backed up away from the systems that normally
   serve it.
6. Plan for term-time pressure, since an outage during assessment periods costs more than the
   same outage in a quiet week.
7. Review access held by education technology suppliers, which often reaches deep into student
   records.
8. Record who authorises a decision to suspend teaching, because that call is made under time
   pressure or not at all.
