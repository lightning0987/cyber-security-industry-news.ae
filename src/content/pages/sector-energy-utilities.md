## Energy and Utilities ranks ninth among UAE sectors with 4 claimed organisations

Energy and Utilities ranks ninth of 13 UAE sectors, well below the average of 8.3 claims per active sector. Four distinct groups appear here, one claim each. The small total should not be read as low exposure, because this sector sits inside critical national infrastructure.

## Energy and Utilities claims are rising from a low base

The sector recorded no claims before 2024. It then saw 1 claim in 2024 and 1 in 2025, and the
partial year 2026 already stands at 2. Leak-site monitoring recorded the first claim on 23
November 2024 and the most recent on 27 April 2026.

APT73, Handala, Lynx and RA World each account for one claimed organisation. The small total
should not be read as low exposure. Energy and utilities operators fall inside critical
national infrastructure. A single incident there carries regulatory consequences that a larger
count elsewhere would not.

## What this means for UAE organisations

1. Confirm your status as critical national infrastructure, because UAE IAS v2 then applies
   across all emirates rather than by emirate.
2. Complete the 39 Priority One controls in UAE IAS v2 first, since the standard makes those
   mandatory for everything in scope.
3. Plan the technical programme separately, given that UAE IAS v2 splits 188 controls into 60
   management and 128 technical.
4. Check whether DESC ISR v3 also reaches you through a Dubai government relationship, bringing
   its 13 control domains with it.
5. Keep operational technology networks separate from corporate networks, and prove the
   separation rather than asserting it.
6. Record how a control system runs without its management layer, because that is the question
   an assessor and an incident both ask.
7. Treat the rise from zero to 2 claims in 2026 as a signal worth watching, not yet as a trend.
