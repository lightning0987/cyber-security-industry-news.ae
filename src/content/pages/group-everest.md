## Everest has claimed 8 UAE organisations since May 2025

Everest ranks third of 46 groups on this tracker and appears across 5 distinct sectors. It reached that position from a standing start. The group had no UAE record at all before May [2025](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2025/).

## Everest went from absent to leading the year in under twelve months

Leak-site monitoring recorded the first Everest claim against a UAE organisation on 8 May 2025.
The group had claimed nothing in the UAE before that date. It then claimed 6 organisations
during 2025 and 2 more in [2026](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2026/).

Those 6 claims in a single year are the highest annual total of any group on this tracker.
Everest reached that position from a standing start. The most recent claim was recorded on 5
August 2026.

[Government and Defense](/uae-ransomware-tracker/government-defense/) absorbed 3 of the 8 claims. Retail and E-Commerce follows with 2 and
Professional Services with 1, with the remainder in other sectors.

## What this means for UAE organisations

1. Assume that a group absent from your sector last year can lead it this year, because Everest
   did exactly that.
2. Note the concentration in Government and Defense, which carries 3 of the 8 claims.
3. Confirm whether DESC ISR v3 applies to you, either directly as a Dubai entity or through a
   government supply contract.
4. Map your estate against the 13 control domains of DESC ISR v3 where that applies.
5. Work through the 39 Priority One controls in UAE IAS v2 if you fall inside critical national
   infrastructure.
6. Record your breach notification path under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law before an
   incident forces the question.
7. Track this page rather than a static threat list, since Everest reached the top three in
   fifteen months.
