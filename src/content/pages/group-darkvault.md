## DarkVault is a double-extortion group with a suspected LockBit connection

DarkVault is a data-exfiltration and double-extortion group first identified in late 2023. The DarkVault profile names finance, professional services, legal and technology as usual targets, mostly in Europe, the UK and North America. Marsad Cyber added DarkVault to the tracker on 11 April 2024. The profile records a suspected connection to LockBit, and lists no aliases, tools or techniques.

## DarkVault's UAE record is small, split across two sectors and limited to 2024

DarkVault ranks fifteenth among groups on this tracker, with two claimed UAE organisations. LockBit 3.0 leads with 10 claims, so DarkVault sits well below the top. Leak-site monitoring, as of 28 November 2026, places one DarkVault claim in [Technology](/uae-ransomware-tracker/technology/) and one in [Healthcare](/uae-ransomware-tracker/healthcare/). Healthcare is absent from the sectors in the DarkVault profile. Both claims fall in [2024](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2024/), on 29 June and 7 November, and DarkVault has made no later UAE claim.

## What this means for UAE organisations

1. Monitor outbound data transfers, because DarkVault is described as an exfiltration group that extorts over stolen data, not only encrypted systems.
2. Include DarkVault in healthcare and technology threat models. The group's UAE claims reached a sector that its own profile does not list.
3. Prepare a UAE PDPL breach notification workflow in advance. PDPL has federal scope and breach notification duties for any organisation processing personal data of individuals in the UAE.
4. Ask suppliers that handle Dubai government data to show evidence across the 13 control domains of DESC ISR v3. The regulation is methodologically aligned with ISO 27001 and NIST CSF.
5. Track DarkVault together with LockBit 3.0 in threat intelligence feeds, since the DarkVault profile records a suspected LockBit connection.
