## What this means for UAE organisations

1. Note that one claim can represent half a sector's record, which is what makes small sectors
   hard to read.
2. Map your obligations under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, which reaches patient,
   student and staff records equally.
3. Establish who owns identity for people who pass through your institution, since accounts that
   outlive enrolment or treatment are a standing exposure.
4. Separate clinical or teaching systems from administrative systems, because the two carry
   different users and different risk.
5. Confirm that records are backed up away from the systems that normally serve them, and that
   restoration has been tested.
6. Plan for peak periods, since an outage during assessment or admission costs more than the same
   outage in a quiet week.
7. Treat a two-claim profile as a sample rather than a pattern, and read the sector pages
   alongside it.
