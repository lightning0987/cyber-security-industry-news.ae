## Professional Services is the most claimed UAE sector at 24 organisations

Professional Services ranks first of 13 UAE sectors. It is also the only sector with claims in
every period on record. It carried 3 claims across 2020 to 2022, then 4, then 7, then 5, then 5.

The count sits nearly three times the average of 8.3 claims per active sector. Firms here should
plan for a steady baseline rather than a passing wave.

## Nineteen groups share 24 Professional Services claims

Professional Services draws 19 distinct ransomware groups, the widest spread of any UAE sector.
[LockBit 3.0](/ransomware-groups-targeting-uae/lockbit3/) leads with 4 claimed organisations and RansomHub follows with 3. Every other group
appears once or twice. No operation has established a durable hold on the sector.

Professional Services is also the only UAE sector with claims in every period on record. It
carried 3 claims across 2020 to 2022, then 4, then 7, then 5, then 5. Firms here should plan
for a steady baseline rather than a passing wave.

## What this means for UAE organisations

1. Treat client data as the asset under threat, because law firms, consultancies and agencies
   hold concentrated records belonging to their clients.
2. Map your obligations under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, which applies federally to
   any organisation processing personal data in the UAE.
3. Check whether you handle Dubai government data as a supplier, because DESC ISR v3 and its 13
   control domains then reach your systems through the contract.
4. Assume a generalist attacker rather than a specialist one, given that 19 groups have claimed
   organisations in this sector.
5. Test third-party and remote access paths first, since professional firms connect outward to
   client environments by design.
