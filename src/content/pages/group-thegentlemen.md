## The Gentlemen is a ransomware-as-a-service group with a multi-platform locker

The Gentlemen is a ransomware-as-a-service group that emerged in July or August 2025, offering affiliates a 90% revenue share. Its Go-based locker targets Windows, Linux, NAS and BSD systems. Threat intelligence reports also track the group as Storm-2697. A compromised C2 server in 2026 reportedly revealed more than 1,570 linked victims worldwide.

## The Gentlemen's UAE record is a single recent run across three sectors

The Gentlemen ranks ninth on this tracker, while LockBit 3.0 leads with 10 claims. Leak-site monitoring, with a snapshot dated 2026-11-23, places all four Gentlemen claims in [2026](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2026/), between 2026-02-06 and 2026-06-20. The group has no earlier UAE history, so the record is one run in one year rather than repeated returns. Those claims are attacker assertions, not confirmed breaches, and they fall across three sectors, so no single industry defines the group's UAE record.

## What this means for UAE organisations

1. Inventory NAS devices, Linux servers and BSD-based appliances alongside Windows hosts, because the Gentlemen's locker targets all four platform types. Keep offline backups for each.
2. Confirm with your designating authority whether UAE IAS v2 applies, especially for [Transportation](/uae-ransomware-tracker/transportation/) operators. The standard sets 188 controls, and 39 Priority One controls are mandatory for everything in scope.
3. Ask suppliers that handle Dubai government data, including professional services firms, to evidence their controls against the 13 control domains of DESC ISR v3.
4. Rehearse the breach notification duties under the UAE PDPL, which applies to any organisation processing personal data of individuals in the UAE, including retailers.
5. Search threat intelligence feeds under both The Gentlemen and Storm-2697, because reports use both names for the same group.
