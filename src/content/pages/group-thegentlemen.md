## The Gentlemen is a ransomware-as-a-service operation that surfaced in 2025

The Gentlemen is a ransomware-as-a-service operation that appeared in July and August 2025, and Marsad Cyber added it to this tracker on 2025-09-09. Storm-2697 is recorded as an alias. Its source profile describes a Go-based locker aimed at Windows, Linux, NAS and BSD systems, sold to affiliates with a 90% revenue share. The same profile reports that a compromised command server in 2026 linked more than 1,570 victims, well above the more than 320 victims the group had publicly claimed.

## The Gentlemen's UAE record is short, recent and spread across sectors

The Gentlemen ranks ninth on this tracker with four claimed UAE organisations. LockBit 3.0 leads the same leak-site monitoring snapshot, dated 2026-11-24, with 10 claims. All four Gentlemen claims fall between 2026-02-06 and 2026-06-20, and none appear in an earlier year or after that last date, so the record is a single run rather than a pattern of returns (see [2026](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2026/)). The Gentlemen's spread across Transportation, Professional Services and Retail and E-Commerce is consistent with affiliates choosing targets by access rather than by industry.

## What this means for UAE organisations

1. Inventory every Windows, Linux, NAS and BSD host exposed to your network, because the locker attributed to The Gentlemen targets all four platform types.
2. Verify that backups, including NAS devices, sit on isolated credentials and have been restored in a test within the last quarter.
3. Map your controls to UAE IAS v2 if you operate in [Transportation](/uae-ransomware-tracker/transportation/). The standard has 188 controls, and its 39 Priority One controls are mandatory for everything in scope. The designating authority decides scope, not the industry label.
4. Review DESC ISR v3 obligations if you are a [Professional Services](/uae-ransomware-tracker/professional-services/) firm handling Dubai government data. The regulation has 13 control domains, and key suppliers handling government data fall within its scope.
5. Rehearse your UAE PDPL breach notification process, because any organisation processing personal data of individuals in the UAE must be able to verify a leak-site claim quickly against its notification duties.
