## The Gentlemen is a ransomware-as-a-service group also tracked as Storm-2697

The Gentlemen is a ransomware-as-a-service group that emerged in July or August 2025 and is also tracked as Storm-2697. Its affiliates receive a 90% revenue share and use a Go-based locker against Windows, Linux, NAS and BSD systems. Source reporting cites over 320 victims in 17+ countries, rising past 1,570 linked victims after a 2026 server compromise. Marsad Cyber's source added the group on 2025-09-09.

## The Gentlemen's UAE record is new in 2026 and spread across three sectors

The Gentlemen ranks ninth on this tracker with four claimed UAE organisations. LockBit 3.0, the most active group, has 10 claims. Leak-site monitoring (snapshot 2026-11-25) recorded the first claim on 2026-02-06 and the latest on 2026-06-20, so every claim falls in [2026](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2026/). The four claims cover three sectors, and [Transportation](/uae-ransomware-tracker/transportation/) is the only one claimed twice, while [Professional Services](/uae-ransomware-tracker/professional-services/) appears once.

## What this means for UAE organisations

1. Prioritise the 39 Priority One controls in UAE IAS v2 if you operate transport infrastructure. Those controls are mandatory for everything in scope, and the scope is set by the designating authority, not by sector label.
2. Inventory every Windows, Linux, NAS and BSD system, including backup storage. The Gentlemen's locker targets all four platform types, so a Windows-only recovery plan leaves gaps.
3. Add Storm-2697 to threat intelligence watchlists alongside The Gentlemen. Affiliates carry out the intrusions, so entry methods will vary and detection should not rely on one technique.
4. Rehearse the UAE PDPL breach notification process with legal and incident response teams. The law sets breach notification duties for any organisation processing personal data of individuals in the UAE, and a claim on a leak site can start that clock for your team.
