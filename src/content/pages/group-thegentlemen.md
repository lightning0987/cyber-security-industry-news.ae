## The Gentlemen is a ransomware-as-a-service operation also tracked as Storm-2697

The Gentlemen is a ransomware-as-a-service group, also tracked under the alias Storm-2697. Source data records The Gentlemen as first seen on 9 September 2025, after the operation emerged in July and August 2025. Affiliates are offered a 90% revenue share and deploy a Go-based locker against Windows, Linux, NAS and BSD systems. A compromised command server in 2026 reportedly exposed more than 1,570 linked victims worldwide.

## The Gentlemen's UAE record is short, recent and spread across sectors

Leak-site monitoring at the 26 November 2026 snapshot ranks The Gentlemen ninth, behind LockBit 3.0 with 10 claims. The Gentlemen has no UAE claims before [2026](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2026/), and all four fall between 6 February and 20 June 2026. Claims reach three sectors, and [Transportation](/uae-ransomware-tracker/transportation/) is the only sector claimed more than once. That spread suggests affiliates choosing targets by access rather than by industry, though four claims is a small sample.

## What this means for UAE organisations

1. Check whether your organisation is a Transportation operator designated under UAE IAS v2, which has 188 controls. If so, evidence the 39 Priority One controls first, because those are mandatory for everything in scope. The designating authority sets scope, not the sector label.
2. Test restoration of Windows, Linux, NAS and BSD systems, because the locker used by The Gentlemen targets all four platform types. Include storage and BSD appliances that endpoint tools may not cover.
3. Ask whether your [Professional Services](/uae-ransomware-tracker/professional-services/) firm is a key supplier handling Dubai government data. If so, map your controls to the 13 control domains of DESC ISR v3.
4. Document a UAE PDPL breach notification procedure. Include how a leak-site claim is triaged as an attacker assertion before anyone treats the claim as a confirmed breach.
5. Track the alias Storm-2697 alongside The Gentlemen in threat intelligence feeds, so that reporting under either name reaches the same triage queue.
