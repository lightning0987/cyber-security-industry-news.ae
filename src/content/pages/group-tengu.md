## What this means for UAE organisations

1. Note that a single claim can represent half a sector's record, which is what makes small
   sectors hard to read.
2. Separate processing and control systems from corporate systems, because production stops
   before recovery finishes.
3. Identify equipment that cannot be patched, then compensate with segmentation rather than
   waiting for a vendor update.
4. Map your obligations under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, which reaches employee and
   supplier records as well as customer data.
5. Confirm that traceability and process records survive an outage, since safety obligations do
   not pause for an incident.
6. Check remote access held by equipment vendors, and time limit it rather than leaving it
   standing.
7. Treat a two-claim profile as a sample rather than a pattern, and read the sector pages
   alongside it.
