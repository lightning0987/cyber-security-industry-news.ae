## FunkSec is an AI-assisted ransomware-as-a-service group

FunkSec sells ransomware access to affiliates and uses AI tooling to lower the technical skill they need. Its data leak site launched in December 2024. The group rapidly claimed more than 85 victims worldwide, across government, technology, finance and education. Each entry is an assertion by the attackers, not a confirmed breach.

## FunkSec's UAE record is one short episode, not a sustained campaign

Leak-site monitoring as of 26 November 2026 ranks FunkSec tenth on this tracker with 3 claimed UAE organisations. LockBit 3.0, the most active group, has 10 claims. FunkSec's claims cover two sectors, [Government and Defense](/uae-ransomware-tracker/government-defense/) and [Technology](/uae-ransomware-tracker/technology/). Every FunkSec claim falls between 24 December 2024 and 19 February 2025, so the record is one short episode.

## What this means for UAE organisations

1. Confirm whether your organisation supplies Dubai government entities. DESC ISR v3 covers key suppliers handling government data, across 13 control domains.
2. Check which of the 39 Priority One controls in UAE IAS v2 apply to you. They are mandatory for everything in scope, and Government and Defense is the sector where FunkSec's claims concentrate.
3. Prioritise patching, multi-factor authentication and tested offline backups. FunkSec's AI tooling is described as lowering the technical bar for affiliates, so assume less skilled operators.
4. Rehearse your UAE PDPL breach notification process. The law applies to any organisation processing personal data of individuals in the UAE.
5. Review the [2026](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2026/) period for new FunkSec claims. A quiet period since February 2025 does not show that the group has stopped.
