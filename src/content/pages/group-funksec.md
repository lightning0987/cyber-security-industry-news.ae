## FunkSec is an AI-assisted ransomware-as-a-service group that surfaced in December 2024

FunkSec runs a ransomware-as-a-service model in which AI tooling lowers the technical skill that affiliates need. The group was added to this tracker on 4 December 2024, around the time its data leak site launched. FunkSec rapidly claimed more than 85 victims worldwide, spanning government, technology, finance and education. The source profile lists no aliases, tools or techniques, so this page describes FunkSec only through its claims.

## FunkSec's UAE record is a short episode, not a campaign

FunkSec ranks tenth on this tracker with three claimed UAE organisations, against 10 for LockBit 3.0, the most active group. FunkSec's first UAE claim appeared on 24 December 2024 and its latest on 19 February 2025. Both dates come from leak-site monitoring, with a snapshot date of 25 November 2026. FunkSec has made no UAE claim since, so the record reads as a single burst rather than a pattern of returns.

FunkSec's claims cover two sectors only: [Government and Defense](/uae-ransomware-tracker/government-and-defense/) and Technology. They begin in [2024](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2024/) and end in [2025](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2025/). Both sectors sit close to the scope of DESC ISR v3 and UAE IAS v2, although the designating authority decides scope, not the sector label.

## What this means for UAE organisations

1. Confirm whether your organisation hosts or handles government data. DESC ISR v3 extends to key suppliers in that position, and its 13 control domains are where an assessor would look for evidence.
2. Check your evidence against the 39 Priority One controls in UAE IAS v2. Those controls are mandatory for everything in scope, including government entities and critical national infrastructure operators.
3. Rehearse your UAE PDPL breach notification process against an unverified claim. A FunkSec leak-site entry is an attacker assertion, so decide who verifies a claim and when notification duties start.
4. Track claims by small groups as well as large ones. FunkSec made few UAE claims but more than 85 worldwide, and AI-assisted affiliate tooling means a quiet period is not proof of absence.
