## FunkSec is an AI-assisted ransomware-as-a-service operation

FunkSec is a ransomware-as-a-service group that this tracker first recorded on 2024-12-04. Its leak site opened in December 2024, and its own entries claim over 85 victims worldwide. FunkSec is described as using AI tooling to lower the technical skill that affiliates need. No aliases are recorded for FunkSec.

## FunkSec's UAE record is one short episode in two sectors

FunkSec ranks tenth on this tracker with three claimed UAE organisations. LockBit 3.0, the most active group, has ten. These figures come from leak-site monitoring, snapshot of 23 November 2026. FunkSec's first UAE claim is dated 2024-12-24 and its latest 2025-02-19. The tracker records nothing from the group since then. The claims sit in [Government and Defense](/uae-ransomware-tracker/government-and-defense/) and Technology only. The group did not spread across many sectors. Its claims fall in [2024](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2024/) and [2025](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2025/) and not in any later period.

## What this means for UAE organisations

1. Confirm with your designating authority whether DESC ISR v3 or UAE IAS v2 applies to you. Sector labels alone do not decide scope.
2. Test your evidence against the 39 Priority One controls in UAE IAS v2 if you are in scope. The standard has 188 controls, and an assessor will look for proof that they operate.
3. Map the 13 DESC ISR v3 control domains to your ransomware defences. Include backup, access control and incident response evidence.
4. Rehearse UAE PDPL breach notification. The law applies to any organisation processing personal data of individuals in the UAE. Decide in advance who judges whether an attacker claim is a confirmed breach.
5. Review logs from December 2024 to February 2025 for signs of intrusion, because FunkSec's UAE claims fall in that window.
