## APT73 is a self-styled APT group that emerged in April 2024

APT73, also tracked under the alias bashe, emerged as a ransomware group in mid-April 2024. APT73 styled itself an Advanced Persistent Threat, a label normally linked to state-backed intrusion rather than extortion. Much of the early public information on APT73 reportedly came from LockBit, not from APT73 itself. This tracker holds no tools or TTPs for APT73, so no technique list exists to map from.

## APT73's UAE record is one episode across two sectors

APT73 ranks eleventh on this tracker with 2 claimed UAE organisations, against 10 for LockBit 3.0, the most active group. Both APT73 claims carry the date 2026-04-27 in leak-site monitoring (snapshot of 2026-11-26), so the record is a single episode, not a campaign. APT73 spread those claims across [Government and Defense](/uae-ransomware-tracker/government-defense/) and [Energy and Utilities](/uae-ransomware-tracker/energy-utilities/), two sectors where critical infrastructure rules often apply. No APT73 claim appears before [2026](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2026/), and the snapshot shows no later one.

## What this means for UAE organisations

1. Treat the APT73 entries as attacker assertions, not confirmed breaches. Check your own telemetry and your suppliers' notices for any sign of exposure.
2. Map your ransomware resilience to the 39 Priority One controls of UAE IAS v2 if you operate critical national infrastructure or a government entity. The designating authority decides scope, not your sector label.
3. Review your evidence against the 13 control domains of DESC ISR v3 if you are a Dubai government entity, or a supplier handling its data.
4. Prepare a breach assessment workflow under UAE PDPL, which applies to any organisation processing personal data of individuals in the UAE. A claim should lead quickly to a decision on notification duties.
5. Test backups and incident response against generic extortion scenarios. With no recorded tools or TTPs for APT73, detections tied to specific tooling have nothing to anchor on.
