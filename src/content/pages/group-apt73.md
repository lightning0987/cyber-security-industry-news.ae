## APT73 is a self-styled APT group first seen in April 2024

APT73 emerged in mid-April 2024 and was added to the source database on 22 April 2024. APT73 reportedly called itself an advanced persistent threat, a label that describes a tradecraft level and was never independently established. Researchers report that much of the early public information about APT73 came from LockBit, a rival operation. The source database lists one alias, "bashe", and no tools or techniques, so any defensive advice here rests on claim data alone.

## APT73 has a one-day UAE record, unlike repeat-claim groups

APT73 ranks eleventh on this tracker with 2 claimed UAE organisations, according to leak-site monitoring as of 24 November 2026. [LockBit 3.0](/ransomware-groups-targeting-uae/lockbit-3-0/), the most active group for comparison, has 10 claims. Both APT73 claims are dated 27 April 2026, and the monitoring shows none in 2024 or 2025 despite the April 2024 origin. No later APT73 claim appears in the snapshot, so a return to UAE targets is not yet evidenced, and the claims remain unconfirmed attacker assertions.

## What this means for UAE organisations

1. Check whether your organisation operates in [Government and Defense](/uae-ransomware-tracker/government-and-defense/) or Energy and Utilities, the two sectors where APT73 made its claims, and add APT73 to your threat model.
2. Map your controls to the 39 Priority One controls in UAE IAS v2, which are mandatory for everything in scope. Scope is set by the designating authority, not by sector label.
3. Gather evidence against the 13 control domains of DESC ISR v3 if you are a Dubai government entity, a semi-government body or a key supplier handling government data.
4. Rehearse the UAE PDPL breach notification path, which applies to any organisation processing personal data of individuals in the UAE. Include the decision step from an unverified leak-site claim to a confirmed incident.
5. Track new APT73 listings, and test each claim against your own logs before treating it as a breach. The single-day pattern in [2026](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2026/) may change with one new entry.
