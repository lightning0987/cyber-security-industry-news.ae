## Retail and E-Commerce ranks fifth among UAE sectors

record therefore fall in the last two periods.

1. Treat customer payment and identity records as the primary exposure, because retail systems
   hold both at volume.
2. Confirm your breach notification path under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law before
   trading peaks, not during one.
3. Test payment and order systems separately from corporate systems, since the two often share
   credentials without sharing controls.
4. Study the three groups with 2 claims each, because repeat activity gives more signal than a
   single appearance does.
5. Rehearse trading through an outage, given that downtime in this sector converts to lost
   revenue within hours.
6. Check that seasonal and contractor accounts are removed on schedule, since retail headcount
   moves faster than access reviews.
7. Confirm that supplier and marketplace integrations hold access under the same review cycle as
   your own staff accounts.
