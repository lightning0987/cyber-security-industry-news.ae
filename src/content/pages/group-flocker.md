## FLocker is a ransomware-as-a-service operation linked to the FSociety brand

FLocker is a ransomware-as-a-service operation, meaning FLocker operators supply the tooling while affiliates run the intrusions. The source profile links FLocker to the FSociety brand and dates FLocker activity to 2023 or 2024 onwards. FLocker is described as targeting Windows and Linux systems through phishing, compromised RDP and exploit kits. FLocker applies a double extortion model, pairing encryption with data theft threats, and has been observed collaborating with FunkSec.

## FLocker's UAE record is a single episode across two sectors

FLocker ranks sixteenth on this tracker with 2 claimed UAE organisations, according to leak-site monitoring as of 2026-11-28. LockBit 3.0, the most active group for comparison, has 10 claims, so FLocker sits far below the leader. FLocker's claims are dated 2025-05-31 and 2025-06-12, one each in [Professional Services](/uae-ransomware-tracker/professional-services/) and [Government and Defense](/uae-ransomware-tracker/government-defense/). FLocker has no claims in [2026](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2026/), so two claims in one episode cannot show a sector preference.

## What this means for UAE organisations

1. Close or restrict internet-facing RDP, because FLocker is described as using compromised RDP for access. Review remote access logs for unfamiliar logins.
2. Test email filtering and phishing reporting, because FLocker is described as using phishing as an entry route.
3. Patch internet-facing software and extend endpoint monitoring to Linux servers, because FLocker targets both Windows and Linux. Prioritise externally exposed systems first.
4. Rehearse a double extortion case against the breach notification duties in UAE PDPL. Decide in advance who assesses whether personal data of individuals in the UAE is involved.
5. Map RDP and phishing controls to the 13 control domains of DESC ISR v3 where in scope. Keep dated records of RDP restrictions and phishing tests as evidence for an assessor.
