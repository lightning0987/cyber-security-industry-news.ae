## Transportation ranks seventh among UAE sectors with 7 claimed organisations

Transportation sits seventh of 13 UAE sectors and carries 6.3% of all attributed claims. The count
runs below the average of 8.6 claims per active sector. The shape of the record matters more here
than its size.

## Four of the seven Transportation claims fall in 2026

Leak-site monitoring recorded the first Transportation claim against a UAE organisation on 26 July
2023. The sector then held steady at 1 claim in [2023](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2023/), 1 in
[2024](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2024/) and 1 in [2025](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2025/).

That pattern broke this year. The partial year 2026 already carries 4 claims, the most recent
recorded on 6 October 2026. A sector that produced one claim a year for three years produced four
in ten months.

Five distinct groups appear in the Transportation record. No single operation accounts for the
rise, which points at opportunity rather than at a campaign against the sector.

## What this means for UAE organisations

1. Treat 2026 as a change of rate, because four claims in one partial year exceed the three
   previous years combined.
2. Check whether UAE IAS v2 reaches you, since transport operators can fall inside the critical
   national infrastructure category.
3. Confirm the designation with the authority rather than inferring it from the sector label.
4. Map supplier access to operational systems, because five separate groups reached this sector
   without a shared method.
5. Read the [2026 period page](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2026/) alongside this one, since the
   sector's recent record and the year's record describe the same claims.

The count is small enough that one further claim moves the sector up the table. Read the direction
here rather than the ranking, because the gap between seventh and fourth place is 5 claims.

Transportation shares the UAE IAS v2 scope with
[Government and Defense](/uae-ransomware-tracker/government-defense/) and Energy and Utilities.
Those three sectors hold 24 claims between them.
