## UAE ransomware claims more than doubled in 2024

Claims rose 125% in 2024 against the 12 recorded in [2023](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2023/). That is the largest single increase on this tracker, and the point at which UAE ransomware activity changed scale. Unlike 2023, no single operation dominated the year.

## Three groups drove the 2024 rise

Stormous claimed 5 UAE organisations in 2024, [LockBit 3.0](/ransomware-groups-targeting-uae/lockbit3/) claimed 4 and RansomHub claimed 4.
Arcus Media follows with 2. No single operation dominated the year in the way LockBit 3.0
dominated 2023.

Professional Services carried 7 claims, [Government and Defense](/uae-ransomware-tracker/government-defense/) 6 and Technology 5. Government
and Defense reached its highest figure of any period in 2024. Retail and E-Commerce and
Manufacturing recorded 1 claim each.

## What this means for UAE organisations

1. Treat 2024 as the inflection point in this dataset, because the count more than doubled
   against the prior year.
2. Note that Government and Defense peaked at 6 claims here and has declined in every period
   since.
3. Read the spread across Stormous, LockBit 3.0 and RansomHub as the start of a broader attacker
   population.
4. Compare your own sector's 2024 figure against its later ones, since sectors diverged sharply
   after this year.
5. Assume improved leak-site monitoring explains part of the rise, which the methodology page
   sets out in full.
6. Avoid attributing the increase to any single cause, because published claims measure attacker
   behaviour and observation together.
7. Note that Stormous, LockBit 3.0 and RansomHub together account for 13 of the 27 claims, so
   the year was concentrated without being dominated.
8. Check whether your sector appears in the 2024 column at all, since Retail and E-Commerce and
   Manufacturing recorded only 1 claim each.
