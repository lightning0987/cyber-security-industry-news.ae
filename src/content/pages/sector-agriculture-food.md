## Agriculture and Food Production ranks twelfth among UAE sectors

Agriculture and Food Production ranks twelfth of 13 UAE sectors, far below the average of 8.3 claims per active sector. Two distinct groups appear here, one claim each. Food production sits closer to national supply resilience than its claim count suggests.

## Both claims fall inside 2025

Leak-site monitoring recorded the first Agriculture and Food Production claim on 24 January
[2025](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2025/). The second followed on 23 October 2025. The sector shows no claims in any other period.
[Medusa](/ransomware-groups-targeting-uae/medusa/) and Tengu account for one claimed organisation each.

Two claims in one year describe no trend. This page exists so the sector breakdown stays
complete and so the figure can be tracked as it moves. Food production also sits closer to
national supply resilience than its claim count suggests.

## What this means for UAE organisations

1. Read 2 claims as a small sample, not as evidence that food production carries low ransomware
   exposure in the UAE.
2. Map your obligations under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, which reaches employee and
   supplier records as well as customer data.
3. Separate processing and cold chain control systems from corporate systems, because spoilage
   starts before recovery finishes.
4. Identify the equipment that cannot be patched, then compensate with segmentation rather than
   waiting for a vendor.
5. Confirm that traceability records survive an outage, since food safety obligations do not
   pause for an incident.
6. Rehearse running a shift without the systems that schedule it, because the first hours decide
   what is lost.
7. Check remote access held by equipment vendors, and time limit it rather than leaving it
   standing.
8. Read this page alongside [Manufacturing](/uae-ransomware-tracker/manufacturing/), since the two sectors share both equipment types and
   recovery constraints.
