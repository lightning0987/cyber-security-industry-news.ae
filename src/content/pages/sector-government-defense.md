## Government and Defense is the second most claimed UAE sector

Government and Defense ranks second of 13 UAE sectors, above the average of 8.3 claims per active sector. Eight distinct groups appear here. The sector carries the heaviest regulatory scope in the country, which changes what an incident costs beyond downtime.

## Government and Defense claims peaked in 2024 and have fallen since

The sector recorded 1 claim across 2020 to 2022 and 1 in [2023](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2023/). Claims then rose to 6 in 2024
and 4 in 2025, before falling to 1 in 2026. Everest and [Stormous](/ransomware-groups-targeting-uae/stormous/) lead the sector with 3 claimed
organisations each, and FunkSec follows with 2.

That shape is unusual on this site. Most UAE sectors rose into [2025](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2025/) or 2026, while Government
and Defense moved the other way after 2024. The 2026 figure covers a partial year and may still
rise before the year closes.

## What this means for UAE organisations

1. Establish which regime you fall under, because Dubai entities answer to DESC ISR v3 while
   critical national infrastructure answers to the UAE Information Assurance Standard.
2. Work through the 39 Priority One controls in UAE IAS v2 first, since those are mandatory for
   everything inside the standard's scope.
3. Map your estate against the 13 control domains of DESC ISR v3 if you are a Dubai government
   or semi-government entity.
4. Note that UAE IAS v2 carries 188 controls in total, split into 60 management and 128
   technical, so plan the technical work separately.
5. Extend the same expectations to suppliers holding government data, because contractual reach
   is how these regulations touch most private firms.
6. Keep evidence that technical controls were tested, not only documented, since assessors ask
   for demonstration rather than description.
