Leak-site monitoring recorded a claim by Black Nevas against a UAE education organisation on 15
October 2026. The group's only earlier UAE claim was recorded on 27 October 2025, in
Manufacturing.

## Education now holds 3 claims from 3 separate groups

The sector carries 3 claimed UAE organisations, which is 2.7% of all attributed claims. It ranks
twelfth of 13 sectors.

Three groups account for those 3 claims, one each. No operation has claimed a UAE education
organisation twice.

Education recorded nothing at all across 2020 to 2024. Its entire record opened on 11 October 2025
and now spans twelve months.

## The group moved sector rather than repeating one

Black Nevas holds 2 UAE claims and they sit in 2 different sectors. The group did not return to
Manufacturing.

That pattern appears throughout this dataset. Of 47 groups on the tracker, 39 hold fewer than 5
claims. Groups in that tail typically reach a sector once and move on.

The reading for an education institution is uncomfortable but simple. A sector with no history is
not a sector with no exposure. The group that reaches it first may have no history there either.

## A small count moves fast

The sector went from 1 claim in [2025](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2025/) to 2 in the partial year
[2026](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2026/). On a base of 3 claims, one more claim changes the sector's
rank.

Read the direction rather than the ranking. Three data points describe no pattern, and this page
exists so the sector breakdown stays complete.

A leak-site claim is an assertion published by an attacker. It is not a confirmed breach, and this
site never identifies the organisation named in one.
