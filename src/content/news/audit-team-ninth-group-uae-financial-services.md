Leak-site monitoring recorded a claim by Audit Team against a UAE financial services organisation
on 13 October 2026. The group had no UAE record before that date.

## Financial Services now shows 12 claims from 9 groups

The sector holds 12 claimed UAE organisations, which is 10.7% of all attributed claims. Those 12
claims are spread across 9 distinct groups.

No other sector on this tracker shows that spread. Most sectors concentrate: one or two groups
account for the bulk, and the rest appear once.

## A wide spread argues against naming a threat

Nine groups across 12 claims means the average group appears here once or twice. Defending against
a named operation would address one claim in nine.

That is the practical reading. The sector is reached by 9 separate operations. The pattern points
at its attack surface rather than at any group's interest in it.

Audit Team now sits in the long tail with a single claim. It joins the 39 groups that have claimed
fewer than 5 UAE organisations and share
[one page](/ransomware-groups-targeting-uae/short-record/) rather than holding individual
profiles.

## The sector record runs longer than any other

Financial Services opened its record on 27 January 2022, earlier than any other sector here. It
then recorded nothing at all during [2024](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2024/) before returning to 4
claims in 2025 and 4 in the partial year 2026.

Read that empty year as a gap in the record rather than as a year of improved posture. The rate
returned to its previous level without anything changing in the sector.

A leak-site claim is an assertion published by an attacker. It is not a confirmed breach, and this
site never identifies the organisation named in one.
