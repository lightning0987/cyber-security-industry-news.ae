Leak-site monitoring recorded a new claim by [n0n](/ransomware-groups-targeting-uae/n0n/) against a Retail and E-Commerce organisation on 2026-11-10. Marsad Cyber rates the UAE attribution as probable, not confirmed. The entry brings the [Retail and E-Commerce](/uae-ransomware-tracker/retail-ecommerce/) total to 11 attributed claims.

## Probable attribution means the UAE link rests on text, not a domain

A probable attribution means Marsad Cyber matched the n0n claim to the UAE by a text mention, not by a .ae domain. In the snapshot of 2026-11-10, 65 of 116 attributed claims are confirmed by .ae domain and 51 are probable by text mention. Marsad Cyber also rejected 80 of 196 raw records as unverified. Readers should treat the n0n link to the UAE as likely but unproven.

## Retail and E-Commerce sits fifth of 13 sectors, above the average

Retail and E-Commerce holds 11 attributed claims, ranking fifth of 13 sectors with activity in the 2026-11-10 snapshot. The average active sector holds 8.9 claims. Retail accounts for 9.5% of all UAE claims.

Eight distinct groups have claimed retail targets since the first entry on 2022-04-25, and n0n is one of them with one claim. The [2026](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2026/) tally for the sector is four claims, level with the four recorded in 2025.

## UAE PDPL gives retailers a reason to check customer data exposure now

UAE PDPL applies to any organisation processing personal data of individuals in the UAE, which includes retailers and online shops. The law has federal scope and carries breach notification duties. Retail and E-Commerce security teams should confirm who decides on notification before an incident, not during one. Teams should also check whether customer records sit in systems exposed to the internet.

The n0n claim is an assertion by the attacker on a leak site, not a confirmed breach. Marsad Cyber does not name the organisation and records only the sector, the group and the date.
