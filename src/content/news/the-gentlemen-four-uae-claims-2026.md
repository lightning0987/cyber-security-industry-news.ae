## What happened

The Gentlemen has claimed 4 organisations in the United Arab Emirates during 2026. The group
had no UAE record in any earlier period on this tracker. Its first claim was recorded on
6 February 2026 and its most recent on 20 June 2026.

That places The Gentlemen ninth among the 46 ransomware groups that have claimed a UAE
organisation. It reached that position inside a single partial year.

## Who is affected in the UAE

[Transportation](/uae-ransomware-tracker/transportation/) accounts for 2 of the 4 claims.
[Professional Services](/uae-ransomware-tracker/professional-services/) and
[Retail and E-Commerce](/uae-ransomware-tracker/retail-ecommerce/) account for 1 each.

The Transportation concentration is the detail worth noting. That sector holds 6 claims in
total across the whole record, so The Gentlemen accounts for a third of it. Most groups on this
tracker spread across sectors with one claim each.

## How this compares

Transportation recorded 1 claim in 2023, 1 in 2024 and 1 in 2025. The partial year 2026 already
stands at 3. The Gentlemen is responsible for 2 of those 3.

A sector that ran at roughly one claim a year has tripled inside an unfinished year. One new
group drove most of that change.

## What to do

1. Establish whether you fall inside critical national infrastructure, because
   [UAE IAS v2](/guides/uae-ias-nesa-controls/) then applies with 39 mandatory Priority One
   controls.
2. Separate operational scheduling systems from corporate systems, since a shared credential
   turns an office incident into a logistics one.
3. Review access held by customs, port and handling partners on the same cycle as your own staff.
4. Define a manual fallback for dispatch, because vehicles and aircraft do not wait for system
   recovery.
5. Treat the 2026 figure as live rather than final, since the year is still running.

## Summary

The Gentlemen has claimed 4 UAE organisations in 2026, its first activity in the country on
record. Transportation carries 2 of those claims, a third of that sector's entire record.
Claims are assertions published by attackers, not confirmed breaches.
