Leak-site monitoring recorded a claim by [Brain Cipher](/ransomware-groups-targeting-uae/braincipher/) against a UAE [Professional Services](/uae-ransomware-tracker/professional-services/) organisation on 17 November 2026. Marsad Cyber rates the attribution to the UAE as probable. The snapshot is dated 19 November 2026.

## Professional Services remains the most claimed UAE sector with 26 claims

Professional Services ranks first among 13 active sectors, with 26 claims in leak-site monitoring as of 19 November 2026. That is 22% of all attributed UAE claims, against an average of 9.1 per active sector. Across the sector, 21 distinct groups have made claims. Professional Services logged 7 claims in [2026](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2026/), against 5 in 2025.

## A probable attribution rests on a text mention, not an .ae domain

Brain Cipher's claim is rated probable because the UAE link comes from a text mention rather than a confirmed .ae domain. The 19 November 2026 snapshot holds 66 confirmed and 52 probable attributions among 118 attributed claims. A further 82 of 200 raw records were rejected as unverified. Readers should treat a probable claim as a lead, not a finding.

## Professional firms face PDPL duties, and some may also fall under DESC ISR v3

UAE PDPL applies to any organisation processing personal data of individuals in the UAE, and it carries breach notification duties. DESC ISR v3 covers Dubai government entities and key suppliers handling government data. It has 13 control domains and is aligned with ISO 27001 and NIST CSF. Scope is decided by the designating authority, not by industry label.

Professional Services firms can check whether a Dubai government contract makes them a key supplier. They can also test their PDPL breach notification procedure against a scenario that begins with a leak-site claim.

A Brain Cipher listing on a leak site is an assertion by the attacker, not a confirmed breach. Marsad Cyber does not name the organisation concerned. The claim is reported by sector, group and date only.
