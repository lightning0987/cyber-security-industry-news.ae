Leak-site monitoring recorded a new claim by [Dark project](/ransomware-groups-targeting-uae/dark-project/) against a UAE organisation in the [Manufacturing](/uae-ransomware-tracker/manufacturing/) sector on 2026-11-10. Marsad Cyber rates the attribution to the UAE as probable, not confirmed. The data snapshot is dated 2026-11-11.

## Manufacturing claims are spread across 9 groups, not concentrated in one

Manufacturing has drawn 9 attributed claims from 9 distinct groups in leak-site monitoring as of 2026-11-11. Each listed group, including Dark project, accounts for 1 claim, or 11.1% of the sector. Manufacturing ranks 6 of 13 sectors and holds 7.7% of all attributed UAE claims. Manufacturers therefore cannot build defences around the tradecraft of a single group.

## Manufacturing claims have held between 3 and 4 a year since 2025

Manufacturing recorded 4 claims in 2025 and 3 claims in [2026](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2026/) up to 2026-11-10, according to leak-site monitoring. The sector had 1 claim in 2023 and 1 in 2024, so the step up came in 2025. One more claim before year end would level 2026 with 2025. Manufacturing sits exactly at the average of 9 claims per active sector.

## UAE PDPL applies to manufacturers that process personal data

UAE PDPL is a federal law covering any organisation that processes personal data of individuals in the UAE. UAE PDPL carries breach notification duties. Sector is only a proxy for scope, and the designating authority decides who is covered.

Manufacturing security teams should confirm who decides whether an incident involves personal data. Manufacturers hold such data in HR, payroll and customer records, even when production systems are the apparent target. Rehearsing that decision and the notification route before a claim appears gives an assessor something concrete to review.

A leak-site entry is an assertion by an attacker, not a confirmed breach, and Marsad Cyber has not verified this claim. Marsad Cyber does not name the organisation concerned, in line with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 34 of 2021.
