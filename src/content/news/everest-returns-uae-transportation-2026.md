Leak-site monitoring recorded an Everest claim against a UAE transportation organisation on 6
October 2026. It is the group's second claim in that sector. The first was recorded on 25 October
2025, which puts almost exactly twelve months between them.

## Everest now holds 9 UAE claims across 5 sectors

The claim takes Everest to 9 claimed UAE organisations and third place of 47 groups on this
tracker. The group had no UAE record at all before May 2025.

Transportation is now one of 5 sectors Everest has reached. Government and Defense remains its
heaviest concentration at 3 claims, while Transportation and Retail and E-Commerce carry 2 each.

## Two groups have claimed UAE transportation more than once

Transportation holds 7 claimed UAE organisations from 5 distinct groups. Two of those groups
account for 2 claims each, Everest among them.

That is unusual on this tracker. Most sectors show one claim per group, which is what
[Hospitality](/uae-ransomware-tracker/hospitality/) and
[Education](/uae-ransomware-tracker/education/) both show today.

A group returning to a sector after a year carries more information than a first appearance. It
suggests the sector stayed reachable rather than that it was reached once by chance.

## What this changes for the sector

Transportation now carries 4 of its 7 claims inside the partial year
[2026](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2026/). The sector produced 1 claim in each of 2023, 2024 and 2025.

A sector that ran at one claim a year for three years is running at four in ten months. That is
the shift worth watching, and Everest is responsible for one of the four.

A leak-site claim is an assertion published by an attacker. It is not a confirmed breach, and this
site never identifies the organisation named in one.
