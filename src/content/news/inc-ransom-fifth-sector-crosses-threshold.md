Leak-site monitoring recorded a claim by INC Ransom against a UAE hospitality organisation on 7
October 2026. It was the group's fifth UAE claim and its fifth distinct sector.

## Six claims, five sectors

INC Ransom holds 6 claimed UAE organisations and carries 5.2% of all attributed claims. It ranks
fifth of 49 groups here.

The sector table lists Professional Services, Technology, Manufacturing, Other and Hospitality,
with one claim in each. The most recent INC Ransom claim is dated 12 November 2026.

Five of the 6 claims fall inside the partial year [2026](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2026/). The
group's UAE record opened on 20 November 2025.

## The claim crossed a publishing threshold

A group earns its own page on this site once it has claimed 5 or more UAE organisations. INC
Ransom held 4 claims before the 7 October 2026 claim and sat in the long tail.

That threshold exists because a record of two or three claims describes an episode rather than a
pattern. The [methodology page](/methodology/) states the rule and why it was raised.

## Hospitality keeps its one claim per group shape

[Hospitality](/uae-ransomware-tracker/hospitality/) now holds 4 claimed UAE organisations from 4
separate groups. Every claim in the sector belongs to a different operation.

The sector recorded nothing before 4 May 2026. Its entire record spans five months, to 7 October 2026, and no group
has claimed a UAE hospitality organisation twice.

For an operator the reading is that sector history offers no protection here. Four unrelated
groups reached the sector within five months, and none of them had reached it before.

A leak-site claim is an assertion published by an attacker. It is not a confirmed breach, and this
site never identifies the organisation named in one.
