Leak-site monitoring recorded a new [Krybit](/ransomware-groups-targeting-uae/krybit/) ransomware claim against a UAE organisation in the [Manufacturing](/uae-ransomware-tracker/manufacturing/) sector on 19 November 2026. Marsad Cyber rates the UAE attribution as confirmed, the higher of its two confidence tiers. The claim is the most recent of 10 recorded for the sector in the snapshot dated 2026-11-19.

## Manufacturing ranks sixth of 13 UAE sectors, with 4 claims in 2026

Manufacturing ranks sixth of 13 active sectors for UAE ransomware claims, with 10 claims and an 8.5% share. The average is 9.1 claims per active sector, so Manufacturing sits just above that figure. Manufacturing recorded 4 claims in [2026](/uae-ransomware-tracker/2026/), matching 2025 and up from 1 claim in each of 2023 and 2024. All figures come from leak-site monitoring, snapshot 2026-11-19.

## Krybit is one of 10 groups, each with a single Manufacturing claim

Krybit holds 1 of the 10 claims in UAE Manufacturing, a 10% share of the sector. Leak-site monitoring shows 10 distinct groups across those 10 claims, so no group has claimed the sector twice. Security teams in Manufacturing therefore face a different group with every claim. Controls that rest on one group's techniques would cover 1 claim in 10.

## UAE PDPL breach notification duties apply to manufacturers holding personal data

UAE PDPL, the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, applies to any organisation processing personal data of individuals in the UAE. The law has federal scope and sets breach notification duties. A manufacturer holding employee, customer or supplier records therefore falls within UAE PDPL scope, whichever group makes a claim. Manufacturing security teams should map where such records sit and who decides on notification.

The Krybit entry on the leak site is an assertion by the attacker, not a confirmed breach. Marsad Cyber has not verified the claim. The project does not name the organisation involved.
